FAQ

How does pricing work? +
We price by volume — how much space your junk takes in our trailer. Final price always confirmed on-site. No surprise charges.

Yes! Same-day service is often available. Call or text (801) 441-5090. For online requests, allow 24 hours.

Cash, credit/debit cards, and checks. Payment collected after the job is complete.

Process

Simple, stress-free junk removal.

From first contact to final sweep — here's exactly what to expect.

01

Call, text, or request online

Start by reaching out however is easiest. Call or text (801) 441-5090, or fill out our online form. Include photos if possible for the most accurate estimate. For same-day service, calling is always the fastest route.

02

Get your free estimate

We'll review your request and provide a clear, upfront starting estimate based on the information and photos you've shared. No vague pricing, no hidden fees — you'll always know what to expect.

03

Schedule your pickup

Choose a day and time that works for you. We offer flexible scheduling Monday through Saturday. Same-day appointments are often available — just ask when you call.

04

We show up & haul it away

Our team arrives on time within your window. We confirm the final price on-site before we start. Then we do all the heavy lifting — quickly and carefully. Tip: have items gathered and accessible to speed things up.

05

We sweep up & you're done

Once your junk is loaded, we sweep the area and collect payment after the job is complete to your satisfaction. We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and checks.

How to prepare for your pickup

  • ✓ Gather all items into one location or pile before we arrive
  • ✓ Point out exactly which items should be taken
  • ✓ Clear a path to items if they're inside the house
  • ✓ Keep pets safely inside during the pickup
